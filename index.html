Sweet Questions & Answers

Are Dinellé ballerina shoes comfortable for all-day walking?

Yes! Every single pair includes a soft memory foam insole and a flexible cushioned heel so you can walk comfortably all day without blisters.

How should I clean satin ballet slippers?

Gently dab any marks with a soft damp cloth and mild soap, then let them air-dry away from direct sunlight to keep the satin shiny and fresh.

Can I exchange my size if it doesn't fit?
